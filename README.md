# Slotwise

A single-screen prototype for planning extracurriculars around rec center availability.

- **Planner**: weekly grid of your classes, clubs, work and booked workouts.
- **Open slots**: sample availability for 5 rec centers (weight rooms, lap swim, bouldering, courts, group classes), checked against your schedule including walking time.
- **Weekly goals + Plan my week**: set sessions per week and get proposed slots that fit; book or discard them.
- Add your own recurring extracurriculars.

No logins or servers. Everything is sample data, saved in your browser's localStorage. Open `index.html` in a browser.
